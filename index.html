What EANOVA actually automates

Each card is a real, named pain point from Indian CA practice — and the specific fix for it.

01 — Reconciliation

GSTR-2B matching

2–3 hours per client, every month, done by eye in Excel.
Fuzzy-matches invoices despite typos and formatting drift; flags true mismatches vs. timing differences in seconds.
02 — Notices

Notice Radar

GST, IT and TDS notices sit unseen across three separate portals, burning response days.
Auto-fetches every notice the moment it's issued and starts a visible countdown, per client, in one calendar.
03 — Deadlines

Compliance Autopilot

Monthly, QRMP and composition clients each follow different due dates, tracked from memory.
Auto-generates the right recurring task for each client's actual filing category, plus DSC-expiry alerts.
04 — Documents

Client Vault

"What documents do you need?" answered over WhatsApp, 15–20 times a day.
Clients upload PAN, bank statements and invoices to a portal; your team is notified only when something actually arrives.
05 — Data entry

Invoice OCR

Vendor, GSTIN and line items typed in by hand from scans and phone photos.
Reads any invoice — PDF, scan, or blurry photo — and validates the GSTIN checksum in about three seconds.
06 — Fees

Aging & Follow-up

No clean view of which clients are overdue on fees, or by how long.
Live aging report with one-tap WhatsApp payment reminders and Razorpay/UPI links.

Reconciliation isn't the only fire

A senior associate's actual Tuesday

This is a composite of how solo and small CA firms across India describe a normal working day.

09:15
Opens GST, Income Tax and TRACES portals one by one, checking each client for a new notice. No single view exists — and the notice's response clock started the moment it was issued, not the moment it's found.
10:00
Assigns pending GSTR-1 and 3B filings to article clerks, cross-checking which clients are on monthly vs. QRMP cycles by memory.
11:30
Responds to 15–20 client WhatsApp messages asking "what documents do you need from me?" — the same question, different clients, every day.
13:00
Manually types vendor name, GSTIN and line items from a stack of scanned invoices into the accounting system, one field at a time.
15:00
Starts the monthly GSTR-2B vs. purchase-register match for a 250-invoice client. Finds three mismatches. Can't yet tell if they're timing differences or real errors.
17:30
Checks a spreadsheet for whose Digital Signature Certificate is expiring this week — because a missed DSC renewal silently blocks every filing behind it.
18:15
Glances at an aging report to see which clients haven't paid this quarter's fee, before writing three more follow-up messages.
