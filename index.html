FAQ

Common questions

Does EANOVA support PDF files?

No — EANOVA currently supports Excel (.xlsx) and CSV files only, for both the Purchase Register and GSTR-2B. PDF reconciliation is not supported. If your file is a PDF, export or convert it to Excel/CSV from the GST portal or your accounting software before uploading.

Is my data safe? Do you store my client's files?

Your Purchase Register and GSTR-2B files are never uploaded to our servers. All reconciliation happens locally, inside your own browser — the data never leaves your device. This means complete data privacy for you and your clients.

Do I need to share my GST portal login?

No. EANOVA never asks for your or your client's GST portal credentials. You simply download the GSTR-2B file from the portal yourself and upload it to EANOVA for matching.

Is there a free trial?

Yes — you can start a free trial with no credit card required. After the trial, plans are billed with manual renewal only (no auto-renewal), so you're never charged without taking action.

What's your refund policy?

Plans are non-refundable once purchased. We recommend using the free trial first to confirm EANOVA fits your workflow before subscribing.
